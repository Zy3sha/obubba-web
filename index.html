Learn about baby sleep

Baby sleep makes more sense once you can see it.

Newborn sleep feels random because it is hidden — scattered across a long day and two tired memories. When you can see the pattern, the 3am questions get quieter. Here are a few of the ideas OBubba is built around.

⏱️

Wake windows, not the clock

Young babies settle best after a set amount of awake time, rather than at fixed clock times. Watch the window and overtiredness has less room to build.

👶

Catnaps are normal

Short, frequent naps are typical in the early months while sleep cycles mature. A 30-minute nap is not a failure — it's a newborn being a newborn.

🌙

Night wakes aren't a verdict

Waking in the night is biology, not a sign you've done something wrong. Seeing the weekly trend — not one rough night — is what makes it feel survivable.

🔥

Wind-down beats willpower

A calm, repeatable run-up to sleep — dimmer light, quieter room, the same few cues — usually helps more than any single trick.

📊

Every baby is different

Charts describe averages, not your baby. OBubba learns from the little person in front of you and adjusts as they grow.

🤝

Shared sleep, shared record

When everyone can see the same nap log, nobody has to hold the whole day in their head or repeat every detail at handover.

Our story

OBubba was built by a mum — me.

I didn't set out to build an app. I set out to survive the newborn fog — the 3am feeds, the "wait, which side was it?", the handover texts that never quite captured the day. The tools I found felt like spreadsheets bolted to a stopwatch. None of them understood that I was tired, that my baby wasn't a chart, and that the person taking over needed context, not a data dump.

So I built the thing I wished existed: an app that helps hold the practical details, reflects your baby's logged rhythm, and lets the people who help actually help. Every feature in OBubba started as a real moment in my own home — the fireflies, the timing guides, Bubba Care handovers, all of it.

OBubba is still mine to look after, and I read every message that comes in. If it makes one night feel a little less lonely, it's doing its job.

FAQ

Everything parents ask before downloading.

How does OBubba predict my baby's naps?
OBubba learns from your baby's logged feeds, naps, wake windows and night sleep. Personal guidance begins around day four, and the picture can become clearer across roughly the first fortnight. These are guides, not guarantees; follow your baby's cues.
What makes OBubba different?
OBubba keeps pregnancy tools, baby tracking, contextual sleep guidance, growth, milestones and shared care in one place. It is designed to reduce handover friction without asking families to track every moment.
Does it lock me into a rigid schedule?
No. OBubba follows your baby's real patterns rather than forcing a fixed routine. Guidance is gentle and responsive, and you can turn predictions and coaching on or off whenever you like.
Is OBubba free?
Yes — OBubba is free to download and start using on iPhone, Android and the web. Premium unlocks deeper personalised insights, reports and extra sharing, but the core sleep and feed tracking are free.
Can my partner, grandparents or nursery see it too?
Yes. Partner Sync keeps both parents on the same live picture in real time, and Bubba Care gives babysitters, grandparents and nurseries a simple, up-to-date care page — so handovers never depend on one tired recap.
Is my baby's data private?
OBubba has no third-party advertising and says it does not sell parent data. Family sharing is invitation-based. Read the current Privacy Policy for details about storage, cloud backup and optional AI features.
What age is OBubba for?
From pregnancy (with a free kick counter and contraction timer) through the newborn months and into toddlerhood — covering sleep, feeds, weaning, growth and milestones the whole way, with preterm correction built in.
