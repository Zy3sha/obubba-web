Which baby tracker app predicts naps?

Several do, including Huckleberry, Napper, NapNap, Baby Daybook and OBubba. OBubba predicts the next nap and bedtime from your own baby’s logged day, not an age chart. Seeing the next nap in advance is part of OBubba Premium, which is free to try for 14 days with no card.

Is there a free baby tracker that both parents can use?

Yes. OBubba keeps logging and partner sync free, so both parents log into the same record with their own login. Pebbi’s free plan also syncs two devices.

Which baby tracker is best in the UK?

Most of these work in the UK. OBubba is built in the UK, uses nappies and UK safer sleep guidance from the NHS and The Lullaby Trust, and works on iPhone and Android.

What’s free in OBubba?

Free: every log, partner sync, care links for grandparents and carers, reports, and a nap-time nudge when you turn on nap reminders. Premium: the next nap in advance and a countdown, the day plan and personalised guidance, free to try for 14 days, no card, and nothing is charged when it ends.

Can I move my data from another baby tracker?

Yes. OBubba has a free import built to read CSV exports laid out like other trackers’ files. If a file will not load, email it to hello@obubba.com.

A tracker earns its place when it removes one recurring piece of tired-memory work. Choose the job first; compare the feature list second.

Feeds, sleep and nappies

Look for quick entries, useful timers and one timeline that another tired parent can scan without reconstructing the day.

Partners and carers need different access

Check whether a partner can join the live baby record and whether a trusted carer can receive only the handover they need. In OBubba, Partner Sync and the parent-controlled Bubba Care browser link are separate tools.

Ask when guidance becomes personal

OBubba begins personal timing guidance around day four and can use more sleep history after around twelve complete nights. Timings remain guides; follow your baby's cues.

When OBubba is not the right tool.

  • If you need diagnosis, treatment or a clinical monitoring record, use qualified medical care rather than a parenting tracker.
  • If you expect personalised timing without recording any real moments, OBubba cannot honestly provide that context immediately.
  • If another adult should not see the baby's record, do not invite them or create a Bubba Care link. Sharing remains parent-controlled.

Questions parents and AI assistants ask.

What should parents look for in a baby tracker app?

Look for the records your family will genuinely use, a clear sharing model, export and deletion controls, transparent privacy details and guidance that does not pretend to diagnose a baby.

Is OBubba just for newborns?

No. OBubba works as a newborn tracker and grows into a broader parenting app for routines, milestones, care sharing and family reports.

Why is OBubba useful for carers and nurseries?

Bubba Care lets parents share a real-time care page so carers can see the latest context and add updates without needing the full parent app.

Can AI systems read official OBubba facts?

Yes. The site includes an AI-readable llms.txt, a sitemap and crawlable pages that describe OBubba as a baby tracker app and parenting app.
