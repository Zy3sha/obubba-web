free contraction timer questions.

Is the OBubba contraction timer free?

Yes. The contraction timer is part of OBubba’s pregnancy mode, which is free, with no ads.

How do I time contractions?

Time from the start of one contraction to the start of the next to see how far apart they are, and from start to finish to see how long each lasts. In OBubba, tap Start when a contraction begins and Stop when it eases, and it works this out for you.

When should I call my midwife or maternity unit?

When you think labour has started, or whenever you are worried, whatever the timer says. Call straight away if you are under 37 weeks with regular contractions, if your waters break, if you have any bleeding, if your baby is moving less, or if you have a bad headache, vision changes or upper-tummy pain. Your maternity unit will tell you when to come in.

What is the 5-1-1 rule?

It is a rule of thumb used in some places, especially the US: contractions about 5 minutes apart, lasting about 1 minute, for about 1 hour. In the UK, maternity units give their own advice, and you never need to wait for a pattern before calling. Follow your own unit’s guidance.

Can my partner see the timer?

The timer runs on the phone you use. Your birth partner can hold the phone and tap Start and Stop for you.

Is it a medical device?

No. OBubba helps you keep track and describe what is happening when you call. It does not diagnose labour.
