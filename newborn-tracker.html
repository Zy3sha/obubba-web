How to track a newborn day in OBubba

Start with feeds and nappies. Add sleep when you can. Nothing has to be perfect.

Step 1

Start a breastfeed

On Track, tap the Breast tile. A live timer starts on the side OBubba suggests from the last feed. Switch side or pause, then tap Stop & log feed.

Step 2

Log a bottle

Tap Feed > Bottle, enter the amount in ml or oz and tap Log feed. A feed during a night sleep is marked as a night feed automatically.

Step 3

Log a nappy in one tap

Tap Nappy. For a wet nappy, tap A little, Normal or Soaked. For a poo, tap Poo or Dirty and add colour and texture if you want.

Step 4

Record sleep and night wakes

Tap "start timer" for sleep. At night, tap the centre pill when your baby wakes, then Resume and say how they settled.

Step 5

Share with your partner

Account > Family & sharing > Share with family > Send invite. You both log to the same live record.

Step 6

Check a worry

Grow > "Is this normal?" covers newborn noises, poos, sleep and crying peaks, with the PURPLE crying explainer and when to get help.

  • OBubba is not a medical device. Logs help you answer questions. They do not assess your baby's health.
  • If your newborn has fewer wet nappies, is feeding poorly, is very sleepy or hard to wake, or has a temperature, contact your midwife, health visitor, GP or NHS 111 straight away. Call 999 in an emergency.
  • Follow safer sleep guidance from the NHS and The Lullaby Trust for every sleep: on their back, in a clear, flat cot or Moses basket, in your room for the first six months.
  • Feed on cue. Feed times in OBubba are a record, not a schedule.

newborn tracker questions.

What is the best app to track newborn feeds and nappies?

Look for one-tap logging, a breastfeeding timer that remembers the side, and one record both parents can see. OBubba does all of this, and every part of it is free for your baby's first two months.

How do I track wet nappies for my newborn?

Tap Nappy on the Track tab, then A little, Normal or Soaked. The day's nappy count shows at the top of Logs.

Can I track which side I breastfed on last?

Yes. The Breast tile starts a live timer on the side OBubba suggests from your last feed, and you can switch sides as you go.

Can both parents log on the same newborn tracker?

Yes. Send an invite from Account > Family & sharing, and your partner joins the same live record on their own phone. Family sharing is free.

Is OBubba free for newborns?

Yes. The whole app is free during pregnancy and your baby's first two months. After that, every log, reports, growth and family sharing stay free, and Premium is optional.
