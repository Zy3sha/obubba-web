OBubba does the consultant’s pattern-reading for you, every day.

OBubba is a baby sleep app that works like a sleep consultant. A consultant reads a sleep diary, connects it to feeds, teething, development and routine, then suggests one change at a time. OBubba does that from the logs a family already keeps: it predicts naps and bedtime from the baby’s own rhythm, names the likely cause of a rough night or short naps, suggests one change for tonight and checks whether it helped. It also offers gentle sleep coaching plans, including night weaning, at the family’s pace. It is not a replacement for medical advice.

What a sleep consultant does, and what OBubba does with your logs.

A good consultant does not hand you an age chart. They look at your baby’s days, spot what is pushing sleep around and change one thing at a time. That is the job OBubba is built to do, from the first few days of logging.

Reads the sleep diary for you

Naps, night wakes, feeds and bedtime sit on one timeline, so the pattern is visible without a spreadsheet.

Predicts the next nap and bedtime

Timing learns your baby’s own rhythm rather than a generic wake-window table, and adjusts as the day unfolds.

Names what is changing sleep

Short naps, split nights, early rising and false starts are matched to what your logs show: timing, feeds, teething, a new food or a development wave.

One change at a time

Each suggestion is a single, small change for tonight, followed by a check on whether it helped.

Gentle sleep coaching plans

Step-by-step plans, including night weaning, that you can pause or stop at any time.

Where a sleep app stops

  • OBubba is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat sleep or health conditions.
  • If your baby seems unwell, is not feeding or you are worried, speak to your health visitor, GP or call 111.
  • Suggested times are flexible guides. Your baby’s cues always come first.

Current parent questions and product evidence

OBubba’s guidance sits alongside, never instead of, national advice on baby sleep and safer sleep.

  • NHS: Helping your baby to sleep — The NHS explains that babies have their own sleep patterns, that those patterns change, and that a health visitor can give individual advice.
  • The Lullaby Trust: Safer sleep overview — Safer-sleep guidance stays separate from any timing suggestion in an app.

baby sleep consultant app questions.

Is there an app that works like a baby sleep consultant?

Yes. OBubba is built to do what a sleep consultant does with a sleep diary: predict nap and bedtime timing from your baby’s rhythm, find what is changing sleep and suggest one change at a time.

Does OBubba replace a human sleep consultant?

For many families it covers the day-to-day pattern-reading a consultant would do. It does not give medical advice, and some families will still want one-to-one support from a professional.

Does OBubba do sleep training?

OBubba offers gentle sleep coaching plans, including night weaning, that move at your pace and can be paused at any time.

How soon does OBubba give personal sleep advice?

Personal guidance begins around day four of logging and uses more of your baby’s sleep history after around twelve complete nights.

Does OBubba look at more than sleep?

Yes. Feeds, teething, weaning, milestones and development waves are all part of the picture, because each of them can change how a baby sleeps.
