Is there a free pregnancy tracker app?

Yes. OBubba is free all the way through pregnancy, with no ads. Several others have a free version with paid extras, as the table shows.

Which pregnancy app becomes a baby tracker after the birth?

OBubba turns into your baby tracker in the same app, keeping your account and settings, and baby Premium is free until your baby is 9 weeks old. What to Expect also includes a baby log.

Which pregnancy apps have a kick counter?

OBubba, Pregnancy+, Sprout, What to Expect, Ovia and The Bump list one. OBubba’s kick counter sets no target number. If your baby’s movements slow down, stop or change, contact your midwife or maternity unit straight away, at any time of day or night, and don’t wait until the next day. Never rely on counting to tell you all is well.

Can my partner follow the pregnancy?

In OBubba, yes, if you choose: partner sharing is off until you turn it on. Pregnancy+ and Flo also list partner sharing.

Does OBubba track my cycle too?

Yes. The cycle tracker is free, in the same app. It is not contraception and should never be used to avoid pregnancy.

Safety

  • OBubba is not a medical device and does not replace your midwife, GP or maternity unit.
  • If your baby’s movements slow down, stop or change, contact your midwife or maternity unit straight away, at any time of day or night. Don’t wait until the next day.
  • In an emergency, call your OB-GYN or 911 in the US, or your maternity unit or 999 in the UK.
