Three steps, and none of them are theirs to pay for.

Questions

Before you buy.

Does the gift renew or charge them later?

No. You pay once. The year ends by itself and nobody is charged again. They are never asked for a card.

When does the year start?

On the day they use the code, not the day you buy it. If they are pregnant or in their first free weeks, OBubba is already free in full, and the gift year starts when that ends. So it is safe to buy before the baby arrives.

What if they already pay for OBubba Premium?

The gift year is added after the time they have already paid for, so nothing is lost. If they pay through the App Store or Google Play, that subscription carries on until they cancel it themselves in their Apple or Google account. We cannot cancel it for them.

How do they use it?

They download OBubba, set up their baby as normal, then go to obubba.com/redeem and enter the code with their OBubba username. The code is not entered in the app.

Can I print it for a baby shower?

Yes. Choose "Send it to me" and you get the card by email. You can print it at A6 or US 4 x 6 inches, or forward the email. The code is on the scratch page the email opens. Print the card from there, or write the code on it yourself.

What if the email goes missing?

Write to hello@obubba.com from the address you bought with and we will send it again.
