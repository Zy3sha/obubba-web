A small group of parents who already use OBubba every day, helping other parents find it.

Not influencers. Not a discount code scheme. Parents who track their baby's sleep at 3am and would tell a friend about it anyway.

Apply now

Or email zyesha@obubba.com directly. Every application is read by me, not a team.

What you get

  • Lifetime Premium. Yours to keep, whether or not you ever share anything.
  • Early access. New features before they ship, and the chance to say when something is wrong.
  • A say in what gets built. Ambassadors are asked first, and it genuinely changes the roadmap.
  • Your own code. A friend who joins with it gets a free month, and so do you.

What we ask

Very little, honestly. Share OBubba where you already are: a baby group, a class, a WhatsApp thread, wherever it would actually help someone. Once, in your own words.

We do not ask for weekly content, scripts, or anything you would not say anyway. You have a baby.

Being straight with you

If you share OBubba because you get something from us, that is an advert, and the law says it has to be labelled as one. Use #ad or say plainly that you are an OBubba ambassador. We would rather lose a post than have a parent feel sold to.

We will never write your words for you, and we will never ask you to say something you do not think is true. If OBubba stops working for your family, say so. That is useful.

Who this suits

Parents who have used OBubba for a while and would recommend it unprompted. If you run a baby group, teach a class, or are simply the person friends ask about sleep, that is exactly it.
