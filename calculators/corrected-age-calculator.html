Why corrected age matters

A baby born eight weeks early who is four months old is closer to a two month old in development. Health professionals assess a premature baby’s development from the due date, not the birth date. Comparing them with babies of the same birth age who arrived at term can make normal progress look slow and push sleep expectations too far.

Frequently asked questions

Until what age do you use corrected age?

Often until about two years old. Some neonatal teams keep correcting for longer, so ask yours what they use for your baby.

Should I use corrected age for wake windows?

Many families find corrected age the better starting point for wake windows and nap counts, because sleep patterns tend to follow development more than birth date. Every baby is different, so watch your baby’s own cues too.

Should I use corrected age for weaning?

Many specialists suggest starting solids around 6 months actual age while also looking for readiness signs. Ask your neonatal team or health visitor for advice for your baby.
