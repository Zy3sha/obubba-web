What a percentile means

On the 25th percentile means that out of 100 healthy babies of the same age and sex, about 25 would weigh less and 75 more. Every percentile line on the chart is healthy. What your health visitor watches is whether your baby follows roughly the same line.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 10th percentile bad?

No. Any percentile can be healthy. The trend over time is what matters, along with how your baby is feeding and developing.

Which growth chart does the UK use?

The UK-WHO charts in the Personal Child Health Record (the Red Book) are based on the WHO Child Growth Standards for babies from two weeks to four years.

My baby dropped a percentile line. What should I do?

Small moves between lines are common, especially in the first weeks. If you notice a bigger change, your health visitor or GP can review it with you.
