Night waking is normal

All babies wake between sleep cycles. What changes with time is whether they can drift back off on their own. A few wakes a night is normal for many babies for much of the first year.

Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions

How many times a night is normal for a baby to wake?

It varies a lot. Many babies wake once or several times a night well into the first year, especially for feeds in the early months.

Why does my baby wake every 2 hours?

Common reasons include a sleep association, being overtired from a late bedtime, hunger during a growth spurt, teething or a new skill. The checker helps narrow it down.

Why does my baby wake 30 to 45 minutes after bedtime?

This is often called a false start and is commonly linked to being overtired or undertired at bedtime. Adjusting the last wake window usually helps.
