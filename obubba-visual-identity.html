What OBubba is.

OBubba

OBubba is a mum-built baby tracker and parent support app, not just a nap timer.

Baby tracking

It tracks feeds, breastfeeding, bottles, pumping, sleep, naps, nappies, medicine, weaning, growth, milestones and notes.

Beyond logging

It includes Partner Sync, Bubba Care handovers, Parent Room support, reports, memory moments and personalised rhythm insights.

Parents awake together

OBubba fireflies represent other parents awake at the same time. The OBubba phrase is: "Fireflies are a symbol of hope."

Baby rhythm clock
