At a glance

OBubba in one minute

OBubba is a parenting support and baby-tracking app for pregnancy and a baby’s early years. It brings everyday care records, shared parenting and practical guidance into one place.

iPhone + Android
Current platforms
Pregnancy onward
Family stage
Parent controlled
Sharing model

Track

Feeds, sleep, nappies, medicine, growth, milestones and other everyday care moments.

Understand

Patterns and guidance begin around day four and become more useful as the family record grows.

Share care

Invited partners share the family record. Parents can also create a private, no-login browser link for a trusted carer.

Approved short description:
OBubba is a baby-tracking and shared-care app that keeps feeds, sleep, nappies, medicine, milestones and care handovers in one place for parents, partners and trusted carers.

Important boundary: OBubba is not a diagnostic or medical tool and does not replace professional advice. Reviewers should describe its guidance as supportive rather than predictive or guaranteed.
