baby milestone tracker questions.

Can OBubba track baby milestones?

Yes. OBubba includes milestone tracking as part of the wider baby tracker experience.

Can new milestones affect sleep?

For some babies, a new skill like rolling or standing comes with a few harder nights. OBubba shows milestones beside sleep so you can see if that is happening.

What if my baby has not reached a milestone yet?

Milestone ages are ranges, not deadlines. If you are worried, talk to your health visitor or GP.

Why track milestones with care logs?

It gives parents context around what was happening in the same period, including feeds, sleep, naps and growth.
