🌮 Egg and cheese breakfast quesadilla

A soft tortilla filled with scrambled egg, a little cheese and spinach, toasted until melty. A Mexican inspired breakfast that is easy to hold and full of protein.

From 10 monthsPrep 5 minCook 8 minContains: Gluten, Egg, Milk

Makes 1 quesadilla, cut into strips

Ingredients

  • 1 small Tortilla wrap (wholemeal)
  • 1 Egg
  • 15g Cheddar (grated)
  • a small handful Spinach (finely chopped)
  • 1/2 tsp Butter (unsalted)

Method

  1. Scramble the egg with the spinach in a little butter until fully set.
  2. Lay the tortilla flat and spread the egg and cheese over half.
  3. Fold the tortilla over.
  4. Toast in a dry pan for 2 minutes each side until golden and the cheese has melted.
  5. Cool and cut into strips.

Serving it to your baby

From 10 months, cut into strips or small triangles. Make sure it has cooled, as melted cheese can stay hot inside.

For the rest of the family

Season adults' with salt, salsa and avocado, and add black beans or peppers.

Storing and reheating

Best eaten fresh. Cooled leftovers can be kept in the fridge for up to 1 day and reheated once until piping hot.

Questions parents ask

Are tortilla wraps OK for babies?

Yes, in moderation. Wraps contain some salt, so look for lower salt versions.

Can I make this for a younger baby?

Yes, from 7 months; cut it into thin soft strips and make sure the tortilla is not crispy.
