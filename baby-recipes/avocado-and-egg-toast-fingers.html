🥑 Avocado and egg toast fingers

Toast fingers topped with mashed avocado and finely chopped hard boiled egg. A quick, nourishing breakfast with protein and healthy fats.

From 7 monthsPrep 5 minCook 10 minContains: Egg, Gluten

Makes 2 slices, cut into fingers

Ingredients

  • 1 Egg
  • half, ripe Avocado
  • 2 slices Wholemeal bread

Method

  1. Boil the egg for 10 minutes until hard, then cool in cold water.
  2. Peel and mash or finely chop the egg.
  3. Mash the avocado and stir in the egg.
  4. Toast the bread lightly and spread with the mixture.
  5. Cut into fingers.

Serving it to your baby

From 7 months, cut into fingers. For younger babies, serve the egg and avocado mash on a spoon.

For the rest of the family

Season adults' with salt, pepper and chilli flakes.

Storing and reheating

Best eaten fresh, as avocado browns quickly.

Questions parents ask

Can babies eat hard boiled eggs?

Yes, and they are a great finger food once mashed or cut into strips.

How long should I boil an egg for a baby?

About 10 minutes until the yolk is fully set.
