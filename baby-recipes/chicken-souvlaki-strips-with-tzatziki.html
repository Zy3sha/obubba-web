🍢 Chicken souvlaki strips with tzatziki

Lemon and oregano marinated chicken strips, grilled until juicy and served with a cool cucumber yoghurt dip. Greek-inspired and perfect for baby-led weaning.

From 9 monthsPrep 15 minCook 15 minContains: Milk, Gluten

Makes about 3 baby portions plus 2 adult

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless thighs Chicken thigh (skin removed, cut into finger-length strips)
  • 1 Lemon (zest and juice)
  • 1 tsp Oregano (dried)
  • 1 clove Garlic (crushed)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 5 tbsp Greek yoghurt (plain full-fat)
  • ¼ Cucumber (peeled, grated and squeezed)
  • 1 tsp Mint (finely chopped)
  • to serve Pitta bread (warmed and cut into strips)

Method

  1. Mix the lemon, oregano, garlic and oil, add the chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Grill or griddle the strips for 10 to 12 minutes, turning, until cooked through with no pink.
  3. Meanwhile, stir the yoghurt, cucumber and mint together.
  4. Serve the chicken with pitta strips and tzatziki for dipping.

Serving it to your baby

At 9 months, give long strips of thigh meat to hold and suck on, or shred them into small pieces. Thigh stays softer than breast. Spoon a little tzatziki on top or let your baby dip.

For the rest of the family

Wrap adult portions in pitta with tomato, red onion, chips and a pinch of salt.

Storing and reheating

Cool quickly and keep the chicken in the fridge for up to 2 days. Reheat until steaming hot, then cool. The tzatziki keeps for 2 days in the fridge. Never reheat the chicken more than once.

Questions parents ask

Are chicken strips safe for babies?

Yes. Large, soft strips of thigh are easier for babies to hold and gnaw than small pieces. Avoid dry or stringy pieces.

Is garlic in tzatziki ok for babies?

A small amount of garlic is fine. Leave raw garlic out of baby's dip if you prefer a milder flavour.
