Delete your account and data

App: OBubba — Baby Sleep Tracker (com.obubba.app) · Last updated: 27 September 2026

You can delete your OBubba account and everything in it at any time. You do not need to email anyone or wait for us to action it.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open OBubba and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account record is removed from our servers first, then everything on the device is wiped.

If you can no longer open the app

If you have lost the device or can't sign in, email hello@obubba.com from the address on your account, or tell us the username on the account, and we will delete it for you. We aim to complete these within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record and username
  • Your family record and backup code
  • Every baby's synced data — sleeps, feeds, nappies, growth, milestones, notes and photos held in sync
  • Your recovery email, if you gave one
  • All data stored on the device itself
A 7-day grace period. Deleting marks the account for removal and signs you out. For 7 days you can sign back in and restore it, in case the deletion was an accident or a partner did it by mistake. After 7 days the data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered by you or by us.

What we keep, and why

  • Purchase records. Subscriptions and payments are handled by Apple and Google, not by us. We keep the minimum record of a purchase where we are legally required to for tax and accounting. We cannot delete a receipt held by Apple or Google — manage those in your App Store or Google Play account.
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics. Counts that are not linked to you or your baby and cannot be traced back to an individual.

Deleting one baby instead of the whole account

If you only want to remove one child, open Account → Babies, choose the baby and remove them. The rest of your account is untouched.

Questions about any of this: hello@obubba.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
