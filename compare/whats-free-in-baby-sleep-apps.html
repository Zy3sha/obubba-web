What’s free in each baby sleep app?

Almost every baby sleep app is free to download, but what you get without paying varies a lot. Here is what 20 apps say their free version includes, in their own words, and whether they list nap predictions at all.

How to choose

  • If you only need to log feeds, sleep and nappies, most apps here do that for free.
  • If you want nap timing, check whether predictions are free or paid in the app you pick, and use any free trial to see whether the timing suits your baby.
  • If two of you are logging, check that sharing with your partner is on the free plan.
  • Export your data before you switch apps, so you keep your own copy.

Questions

Is there a free baby sleep app with nap predictions?

Some apps say their nap or sleep predictions are free and others keep them in a paid plan. Pixy says its sleep predictions are always free. NapNap, nappi, Pebbi, Robin Baby and Snug say predictions are part of their paid plans. In OBubba, logging is free and the exact nap and bedtime timing is Premium. Check each app’s own page below, because plans change.

Is OBubba free?

OBubba is free to download. Core tracking always stays free, including every log, growth, milestones, reports and family sharing with your partner. Premium adds exact nap and bedtime timing, Luna, daily plans and guidance, and more than one baby. On the US App Store Premium is $7.99 monthly, $79.99 yearly, $129.99 lifetime, and current prices are shown in the app.

Which baby tracker apps let both parents log for free?

Several apps include sharing on their free plan, for example OBubba (family sharing), Huckleberry (sync with multiple caregivers), nappi (family sync), Pebbi (sync between 2 devices) and Robin Baby (up to 3 caregivers), according to their own pages. Others, such as Pixy and Snug, list caregiver sharing as paid.
