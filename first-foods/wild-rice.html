🍚 Wild rice for babies: when and how to serve it

Can babies eat wild rice? Yes, from about 6 months if it is cooked very soft. Wild rice is actually the seed of a water grass, with a nutty taste and lots of fibre, but its chewy, firm grains need extra cooking and mashing for young babies.

From 6 monthsTake care with shape and texture

Good source of: Protein, Fibre, Zinc, Manganese

How to serve wild rice by age

6 to 9 months

Simmer wild rice for 50 to 60 minutes, longer than the packet suggests, until the grains split open and are soft. Blend or mash with softer white rice, vegetables and a little milk. For finger food, mix into soft rice balls that hold together.

9 to 12 months

Offer soft-cooked wild rice mixed with white or brown rice in risotto, pilaf or with stews. Your baby can practise picking up clumps.

12 months and up

Serve wild rice in salads, soups and stuffings with the family, mixed with softer grains.

Choking safety

Undercooked wild rice is hard and chewy, and loose firm grains can be tricky for babies. Cook until split and soft, and mix with stickier foods so it holds together.

Gagging is common and noisy while babies learn to eat. Choking is usually silent. Always stay with your baby while they eat, sitting upright. How to help a choking baby (NHS).

Good to know

  • Cool and refrigerate leftover cooked rice within an hour and reheat only once, until steaming hot.
  • Wild rice blends often include seasoning sachets that are salty; use plain rice.

Easy ideas

  • Wild and white rice with mashed squash
  • Wild rice and chicken soup
  • Soft rice balls with wild rice and peas

Questions parents ask

Can babies eat wild rice?

Can babies eat wild rice? Yes, from about 6 months if it is cooked very soft. Wild rice is actually the seed of a water grass, with a nutty taste and lots of fibre, but its chewy, firm grains need extra cooking and mashing for young babies.

Is wild rice real rice?

No, it is the seed of an aquatic grass, but it cooks and is used much like rice.

Does wild rice contain arsenic?

It is generally lower in arsenic than rice, and as part of a varied diet it is fine for babies.

Is wild rice gluten-free?

Yes, plain wild rice is naturally gluten-free.
