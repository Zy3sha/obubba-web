🫘 Kidney beans for babies: when and how to serve it

Can babies eat kidney beans? Yes, from about 6 months. Kidney beans are rich in plant protein, iron and fibre. Tinned beans are already cooked and easy to use, but dried kidney beans must be soaked and boiled properly, as raw or undercooked ones can make people very unwell.

How to serve kidney beans by age

6 to 9 months

Rinse tinned kidney beans (no added salt or sugar), then slip off the skins if you like and mash or blend them with a little water, breast milk or formula. Stir into rice, sweet potato or a mild tomato sauce. For finger food, squash each bean flat between your fingers.

9 to 12 months

Offer squashed beans to practise the pincer grip, or serve a mild chilli with beans mashed into the sauce. Kidney bean and vegetable patties are good finger food.

12 months and up

Serve whole soft beans in chilli, stews and burrito bowls with the family. Keep the spice mild and keep checking they squash easily.

Choking safety

Whole beans are round and firm-skinned, so for young babies mash them or squash each one flat. From around a year, whole soft beans are usually fine once your child chews well.

Gagging is common and noisy while babies learn to eat. Choking is usually silent. Always stay with your baby while they eat, sitting upright. How to help a choking baby (NHS).

Good to know

  • Never serve raw or undercooked dried kidney beans. Soak for at least 12 hours, then boil hard for at least 10 minutes before simmering until soft.
  • Choose tins in water with no added salt or sugar, and rinse well.

Easy ideas

  • Mild bean chilli with rice
  • Kidney bean and sweet potato mash
  • Kidney bean and cheese quesadilla strips

Questions parents ask

Can babies eat kidney beans?

Can babies eat kidney beans? Yes, from about 6 months. Kidney beans are rich in plant protein, iron and fibre. Tinned beans are already cooked and easy to use, but dried kidney beans must be soaked and boiled properly, as raw or undercooked ones can make people very unwell.

Are tinned kidney beans safe for babies?

Yes. They are already fully cooked. Rinse them and choose tins without added salt or sugar.

Do kidney beans cause wind?

Beans can make babies a bit windy at first. Start with small amounts and the gut usually adjusts.

Can babies eat baked beans?

Baked beans are usually haricot beans, and most contain salt and sugar. Reduced-salt, reduced-sugar versions are fine occasionally from about 6 months.
