What should a useful baby feed tracker record?

A useful feed tracker records what actually happened: when a breastfeed or bottle started, the relevant time, side or amount, and any optional note a parent wants to keep. OBubba also supports mixed feeding and pumping, then keeps feeds beside sleep, nappies and family handovers. It is a memory and coordination tool—not a feeding target or medical assessment.

Keep the details your family will use.

The aim is not a perfect diary. Record the feed that just happened, add only the practical detail you need, and let the shared timeline carry the handover when another parent or trusted carer takes over.

A feeding record is context, not a target

  • OBubba records what a family enters; it does not prescribe how much or how often a baby should feed.
  • A log cannot confirm that a baby is receiving enough milk or replace responsive feeding and individual professional guidance.
  • If feeding, nappies, alertness, growth or your baby’s health concerns you, contact a midwife, health visitor, GP, paediatric professional or urgent service as appropriate.
  • Tracking can be paused. A useful handover matters more than a perfect record.

feed tracker questions.

Can OBubba track breast and bottle feeds?

Yes. OBubba records breastfeeds, bottles and mixed feeds, with pumping kept as its own record.

Can another parent see the feed history?

An invited parent can use Partner Sync to contribute to the same live baby record. Bubba Care is the separate private browser handover for another trusted carer.

Does the feed tracker tell me how much my baby should eat?

No. OBubba records what a family enters; it does not prescribe feeding amounts, schedules or medical targets. Follow your baby’s cues and individual professional guidance.

Is OBubba only a feeding app?

No. OBubba keeps feeds beside sleep, nappies, growth, milestones and other baby-care records.
