19 to 24 Month Old Sleep Schedule: One Nap, Wake Windows and Bedtime

A 19 to 24 month old sleep schedule: one midday nap, wake windows of about 5 to 6 hours, bedtime between 6:30pm and 8:30pm, a sample day and gentle fixes.

Between 19 and 24 months most toddlers need about 11 to 14 hours of sleep in 24 hours, with 1 nap of about 1 to 3 hours in the middle of the day. Wake windows are about 5 to 6 hours, and bedtime usually lands between 6:30pm and 8:30pm.

These ranges come from the sleep tables inside the OBubba app. They are a guide to start from, not a target your toddler has to hit.

At a glance

  • Total sleep in 24 hours: 11 to 14 hours
  • Naps: 1 nap a day
  • Nap length: about 1 to 3 hours
  • Wake windows: about 5 to 6 hours
  • Total day sleep: about 2 to 3 hours
  • Bedtime window: 6:30pm to 8:30pm

A sample day

  • 7:00am Wake up for the day, milk and breakfast
  • 12:30pm Nap (2 hours), after lunch
  • 2:30pm Awake, snack
  • 7:30pm Asleep for the night (bedtime routine from about 7pm)

What's changing at this age

Language is exploding, and so is independence. Many toddlers go through a sleep shake-up around 18 months: more protest at bedtime, a few unsettled nights, sometimes a refused nap. It usually passes in a week or two if you keep the day steady.

Very few toddlers are ready to drop their last nap at this age. A skipped nap now and then is normal; on those days, go earlier at bedtime.

Common sleep problems and gentle fixes

Refusing the nap

Keep offering it at the same time, in a calm, dark room. Quiet time in the cot still helps. Bring bedtime earlier on no-nap days.

Bedtime stalling

"One more story" is a toddler classic. A short, predictable routine with clear steps, and the same calm answer each time, works better than negotiating.

Early waking

Check the nap isn't running too late into the afternoon and that bedtime isn't drifting. Our bedtimes by age post shows what we see in OBubba families.

Climbing out of the cot

Lower the mattress to its lowest setting and keep the cot clear. A toddler sleeping bag can help.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a 2 year old nap?

Often 1 to 2 hours, usually starting around midday.

What time should a 2 year old go to bed?

Somewhere between 6:30pm and 8:30pm suits many 2 year olds.

When do toddlers drop their last nap?

Usually well after 2, often between about 2 and a half and 4 years old.

Safety and a gentle reminder

Every sleep should follow safer sleep guidance for your child's age. See The Lullaby Trust safer sleep advice and NHS: helping your child to sleep. The ranges are guides, not targets. If you are worried about your child's sleep, feeding or health, speak to your health visitor or GP.
