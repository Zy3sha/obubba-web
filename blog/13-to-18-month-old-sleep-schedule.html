A 13 to 18 month old sleep schedule: the move from 2 naps to 1, wake windows, bedtime between 6pm and 8:30pm, sample days for both, and what OBubba families log.

Between 13 and 18 months most toddlers need about 11 to 14 hours of sleep in 24 hours. This is the age of the big move from 2 naps to 1: many start the period on 2 naps with wake windows of about 3 hours 15 minutes to 4 hours, and finish it on 1 longer midday nap. Bedtime usually lands between 6pm and 8:30pm.

These ranges come from the sleep tables inside the OBubba app. They are a guide to start from, not a target your toddler has to hit.

At a glance

  • Total sleep in 24 hours: 11 to 14 hours
  • Naps: 2 naps at the start of this age, moving to 1 nap for most by 18 months
  • Wake windows on 2 naps: about 3 hours 15 minutes to 4 hours
  • Wake windows on 1 nap: about 5 hours, a little less in the first weeks
  • Total day sleep: about 2 to 3 hours
  • Bedtime window: 6pm to 8:30pm

A sample day on 2 naps

  • 7:00am Wake up for the day, milk and breakfast
  • 10:30am Nap 1 (1 hour)
  • 11:30am Awake, lunch
  • 3:15pm Nap 2 (45 minutes)
  • 4:00pm Awake, tea
  • 7:30pm Asleep for the night (bedtime routine from about 7pm)

A sample day on 1 nap

  • 7:00am Wake up for the day, milk and breakfast
  • 12:15pm Nap (2 hours), after lunch
  • 2:15pm Awake, snack
  • 7:00pm Asleep for the night (bedtime routine from about 6:30pm)

On the first weeks of 1 nap, an earlier bedtime helps: the afternoon is long, and an overtired toddler often wakes earlier the next morning.

The move from 2 naps to 1

The 2 to 1 nap transition usually happens somewhere between 13 and 18 months. Look for signs that last about two weeks, not one bad day:

  • the morning nap takes a long time to settle, or is very short;
  • the afternoon nap is being skipped or pushes bedtime very late;
  • your toddler copes happily with a longer morning.

When the signs are there, move the single nap gradually later, by about 15 to 30 minutes every few days, until it sits around midday. Some toddlers go back and forth for a week or two, and that's normal. Our nap transitions guide and is my baby ready to drop a nap? go into more detail.

Common sleep problems and gentle fixes

Early waking after dropping to 1 nap

Usually a sign the afternoon awake time is too long for now. Bring bedtime 15 to 30 minutes earlier for a week or so.

Bedtime battles

Toddlers test limits. A short, predictable routine and a calm, consistent response help more than a longer routine.

Night waking around walking and new words

Big developmental leaps can unsettle sleep for a few nights. Keep night responses calm and boring, and it usually passes.

Bedtime creeping later

In OBubba families, later bedtimes rarely buy the morning back. Our bedtimes by age post shows what we see.

Frequently asked questions

How many naps does a 15 month old need?

Many 15 month olds are still on 2 naps, and many have moved to 1. Both are normal at this age.

When do toddlers go to 1 nap?

Usually between 13 and 18 months. Watch for signs that last about two weeks before making the change.

What time should an 18 month old go to bed?

Somewhere between 6:30pm and 8:30pm suits many 18 month olds, and an earlier bedtime helps in the first weeks on 1 nap.

How long should a toddler nap on 1 nap?

Often 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes, usually starting around midday.

Safety and a gentle reminder

Every sleep should follow safer sleep guidance for your child's age. See The Lullaby Trust safer sleep advice and NHS: helping your child to sleep. If your child was born prematurely, use their corrected age when reading these ranges. The ranges are guides, not targets. If you are worried about your child's sleep, feeding or health, speak to your health visitor or GP.
