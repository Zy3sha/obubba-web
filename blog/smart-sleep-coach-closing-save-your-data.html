Why is Smart Sleep Coach by Pampers closing? What Pampers has said, and how to save your baby's data

Smart Sleep Coach by Pampers closes on 30 November 2026. What Pampers has and has not said about why, how to ask for your baby's full data by 16 November, refunds, and what to look for next.

If you have been using the Pampers sleep coach app, Smart Sleep Coach by Pampers, you may have seen the news: the app is closing on 30 November 2026. This guide covers what Pampers has said about why, how to keep your baby's full records before the app goes, and what to look for if you want to keep tracking somewhere else. The first half is useful whichever app you choose next.

The one thing to do today: ask Smart Sleep Coach support for your baby's full data. Pampers' help centre says the full record is sent by hand on request, as a CSV or JSON file, and that it can take up to two weeks. Ask by 16 November 2026. That date is our reading of Pampers' own stated processing time, not a guarantee, so ask today if you can. How to ask is in step 1 below.

Everything below about Smart Sleep Coach comes from Pampers' closure page and its help centre article Can I Export My Baby's Sleep Data? (updated 2 July 2025), both checked on 13 November 2026.

Why is Smart Sleep Coach closing?

Pampers has not given a reason. Its closure page sets out the dates, refunds and how to save your data, and says Pampers' commitment to supporting your baby's development continues, but it does not say why the app is ending. We have not found a reason from Pampers anywhere else, so we will not guess at one. If Pampers publishes one, we will add it here.

When Smart Sleep Coach closes

Pampers says the app stays available until 11:59pm EST on 30 November 2026. From 1 December 2026 you will no longer be able to open it. That is 11:59pm Eastern time, which is 4:59am on 1 December in the UK, so if you are outside the US it is safest to finish everything on 30 November.

The app can no longer be downloaded, so if you delete it or change phone before then, you may not be able to get back in.

Refunds and charges

Pampers says it has stopped all auto-renewals, so you will not be charged anything new. If you have an annual subscription with time left after 30 November 2026, Pampers says a pro-rated refund will be issued automatically, and processing times may vary. You keep full access until the closure date.

Step 1: ask Pampers support for your full data

Pampers' help centre says the app has no built-in export of your full record, but support will send your baby's sleep data as a CSV or JSON file on request, and it may take up to two weeks. The app closes on 30 November, so ask by 16 November.

  1. Open the Smart Sleep Coach help centre and choose Submit a request.
  2. Use the email address your Smart Sleep Coach account uses.
  3. Copy this message, or write your own:

> Hello, Smart Sleep Coach is closing on 30 November, so please send me my baby's full sleep and feed history as a CSV file (JSON is fine too) for the account that uses this email address. Thank you.

  1. When the file arrives, save it somewhere you control and check it opens.

Step 2: also download the weekly and monthly summary

Pampers' closure page says: "Go to the Progress screen, open the Patterns view, and you can download your weekly and monthly data from there."

  1. Open Smart Sleep Coach and go to the Progress screen.
  2. Open the Patterns view.
  3. Download your weekly and monthly data and save it somewhere you control.

Pampers describes this as weekly and monthly data. Our reading is that it is a summary of patterns, not a record of each nap and feed, which is why the full file from support matters.

Step 3: screenshot your schedule and sleep plan

Pampers says your baby's personalised schedule and sleep plans will not be accessible after the closure, and recommends taking screenshots of them and of anything else you want to keep. Scroll through each part of the plan and capture every screen, then put the screenshots in an album so they are easy to find later.

Step 4: keep the originals safe

Whatever you do next, keep your files and screenshots as your own record. They are useful for your own reference, for a health visitor or doctor appointment, or for bringing into another app.

What to look for in your next app

There are plenty of baby sleep and tracking apps, and Huckleberry, Napper, Nara Baby and Baby Tracker are among the ones parents often mention. Whichever you look at, these questions are worth asking:

  • Can it bring in your history? Check whether it can import a file from another app, and what happens if your file is not a format it knows.
  • Can you get your data out again? An export button means you are never stuck if an app changes or closes.
  • What is free? Look for what you can log without paying, and read the trial terms. Some trials start a paid subscription automatically when they end.
  • Can your partner log too? Shared logging keeps night feeds and naps in one place.
  • Does it adapt to your baby? Some apps give fixed age-based schedules; others adjust as you log.
