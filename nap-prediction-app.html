How does OBubba predict the next nap?

On day one it starts from an age-based window. As you log naps and night sleep it learns your baby’s own awake times, then moves the next nap and bedtime when today runs long or short. If a predicted nap does not happen, it offers a calm fallback: try again, a rescue nap, quiet rest or an earlier bedtime.

How accurate are nap predictions?

They are estimates. Every app’s predictions improve with consistent logging, and no app can promise a nap. Watch your baby’s sleepy cues and adjust.

Does it work for newborns?

Yes, from birth. In the first weeks predictions lean on age-based windows, because newborn sleep is irregular.

Can both parents see the next nap?

Yes. Both parents log into one shared record with their own login, and with Premium both phones show the next nap.

Can grandparents see it?

A care link shows how the day has gone, with no app or login. With Premium, it shows your baby’s own next nap too.

Predictions that learn your baby’s rhythm.

OBubba is a baby nap prediction app. It predicts the next nap and bedtime from the baby’s own logged rhythm rather than a fixed age chart, and updates the plan as the day unfolds, for example after a short nap. Personal predictions begin around day four of logging and get sharper over about twelve nights. Suggested times are flexible guides; the baby’s cues come first.
