Smart Sleep Coach by Pampers is closing: what to do next

Checked on Pampers’ closure page, 2026-11-13

Smart Sleep Coach by Pampers closes on 30 November 2026. Your baby’s full history comes from Pampers support, not the in-app download, and a request can take up to two weeks. So whatever you use next, ask for it first.

First: ask Pampers for your full data

Ask by 16 November 2026. Pampers’ help centre says a data request can take up to two weeks, and the app closes on 30 November. The 16 November date is our reading of Pampers’ own stated processing time, not a guarantee, so ask today if you can.

  1. Ask Smart Sleep Coach support for your full data, today. Pampers’ help centre says the app has no built-in export of your full record, but support will send your baby’s sleep data as a CSV or JSON file on request, and it may take up to two weeks. Use Submit a request on the Smart Sleep Coach help centre and copy the message below.
  2. Also download the weekly and monthly summary. In Smart Sleep Coach, go to the Progress screen, open the Patterns view, and download your weekly and monthly data from there.
  3. Screenshot your schedule and sleep plans. Pampers says your baby’s personalised schedule and sleep plans will not be accessible after the closure, so capture every screen you want to keep.
  4. Keep both somewhere safe. Save the file and the screenshots to your Files app, a cloud drive or an email to yourself, and check the file opens before 30 November.

A message you can copy:

Hello, Smart Sleep Coach is closing on 30 November, so please send me my baby’s full sleep and feed history as a CSV file (JSON is fine too) for the account that uses this email address. Thank you.

Submit a request on the Smart Sleep Coach help centre · Read Pampers’ export article

Pampers says the app stays available until 11:59pm EST on 30 November 2026 (4:59am on 1 December in the UK) and cannot be opened from 1 December. It has stopped all auto-renewals, so there are no new charges, and annual subscribers with time left after 30 November get a pro-rated refund automatically. The app can no longer be downloaded, so avoid deleting it until you have saved everything. Read Pampers’ closure page.

Bring your history to OBubba

OBubba has a free import. In OBubba, open the Account tab, tap “Memories & data” to open it, then tap IMPORT DATA. The full CSV or JSON file from Pampers support is the one to bring: the import is built to read any CSV with a date or time column and a type column, as well as OBubba’s own export and files laid out like Huckleberry’s and Nara Baby’s. It shows you a preview before anything is added.

We have not seen a Smart Sleep Coach file yet, so if yours does not load, or the preview does not look right, email it to hello@obubba.com and we will bring it across.

Pampers describes the in-app download as weekly and monthly data. Our reading is that it is a summary of patterns, not a record of each nap and feed, so on its own it cannot rebuild a day-by-day history in OBubba. If the summary is all you have, you can still start fresh: add your baby’s age and today’s routine, and OBubba suggests naps and bedtime from the first day.

We use your file only to bring your baby’s history into your OBubba account, and we delete it from our inbox and machines once it is imported or after 30 days, whichever comes first.
