What is OLife?

OLife balances your whole life — the roles and people that matter, not just tasks. Oli, your companion, plans a gentle day around your real commitments, holds the things you can't hold in your head, and never, ever shames you.

Common questions

Is my data private? Yes — everything lives on your phone. See the privacy policy.

How do I link with my partner? Both open OLife → Together → "Link by touch", and hold your phones together. Your Olis will high-five. You can also share a 6-letter code instead.

How do I delete my account? Together → Delete account. Your on-phone data stays with you.

Why does OLife ask for calendar access? So your day plan fits around real appointments. It's read-only and optional.
